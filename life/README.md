# Life pages

Public music content is a manually reviewed, deliberately limited presentation of works and theatre experiences, not a personal library export. Adding an item to a private library, playing it, or matching catalogue metadata is not permission to publish it. Never commit a full playlist, private library, listening history, play counts, inferred favourites, or exported source data. Only owner-approved individual public selections may be added. Local import helpers may generate private candidates outside this repository; never copy unreviewed output into the site.

Travel uses the explicitly requested approximate flight overview. Detailed itinerary points, exact visit coordinates, date-by-date sequences, bookings and lodging data must remain private. Public travel impressions are small editorial excerpts, not a comprehensive visited-place list. OpenStreetMap attribution and schematic route notices are retained. Any future personal photos or detailed venue lists require explicit review before publication.

## Editorial presentation
The music page is an editorial selection of explicitly discussed musicals and public catalogue recording references, not a listening or ownership record. Other music is represented only by broad genre labels. A particular version shown is an illustration of the work, not evidence that the owner listened to or owns that version. The travel page combines schematic flight overview with three cultural impressions, without an itinerary index. No new private library or itinerary records may enter public files.
